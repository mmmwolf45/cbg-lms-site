IOSH Level 3 Certificate

The IOSH Level 3 Certificate provides practical knowledge and skills to identify workplace hazards, manage risks, and support effective occupational health and safety practice.

  • 5m
  • 8 Sections
  • 43 Lessons

This course includes

  • IOSH Approved Study Centre 1003
  • Live online classes with CBG trainers
  • Ofqual-regulated Level 3 qualification
  • 4 bonus certificates included
  • 8 CBG Field Guides from day 1
  • 2 IOSH assessments, taken online
