Overview

About the course

Structural engineers make sure buildings stand up safely and economically. They work out the loads, model the building, analyse it and design every beam, slab, column, wall and foundation, then issue the drawings the site builds from.

This course follows that workflow on a real multi-storey RCC building. You start with the framing plan and the soil report, calculate gravity, wind and seismic loads, build and check the model in ETABS, and then design each element by hand, checking the software as a practising engineer would.

Who teaches it

The course is run by Carbon Blue Global, an engineering services consultancy headquartered in Doha, Qatar, with structural, MEP, quantity surveying, BIM and drafting teams working on projects across the Middle East, Europe and India.

What You Will Learn

  • Explain the structural engineer's role and the design process from architectural drawings to GFC drawings
  • Prepare a framing plan and read a soil investigation report
  • Model, load and analyse a building in ETABS and STAAD.Pro
  • Calculate gravity, wind and seismic loads to IS 875 and IS 1893, with BS and ASCE 7 comparisons
  • Set up load combinations and cracked-section stiffness modifiers
  • Run model checks for drift, displacement and irregularities, and static and dynamic analysis
  • Verify software output with quick hand calculations
  • Design RCC beams to IS 456, BS 8110 and ACI 318
  • Design one-way, two-way and cantilever slabs
  • Design columns, staircases and shear walls, with code-compliant detailing
  • Design isolated and combined footings, piles and pile caps
  • Design cantilever retaining walls and rectangular water tanks
