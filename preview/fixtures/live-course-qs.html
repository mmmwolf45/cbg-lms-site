Overview

About the course

Quantity surveyors keep construction projects on budget. They measure the work, price it, manage the contract and track every change through to handover. This course takes you through that whole job in 8 modules, in the order you meet it on a real project:

  • Module 1: QS Foundations. The QS role, construction basics, the project workflow, and Excel and AutoCAD for QS.
  • Module 2: Measurement and BOQ. POMI, quantity takeoff, PlanSwift, and BOQs for civil, fit-out and roadworks, plus bar bending schedules.
  • Module 3: Estimating and Cost Planning. Rate analysis, estimates and detailed budgets, and value engineering.
  • Module 4: Tendering and Contracts. Tendering, contract types and FIDIC, GCC and SCC, work orders and purchase orders.
  • Module 5: Post-Contract Billing. RA bills, final bills and certificates of payment, variations, provisional sums and prime cost.
  • Module 6: Cost Control and Planning. Cash flow, cost reports, project scheduling in Primavera P6, and 3M management.
  • Module 7: Closeout and Live Project. Project closeout and handover, a Middle East live project and an industry interaction session.
  • Module 8: Career Launch. Your CV and LinkedIn profile, CV reviews, interview preparation and placement assistance.

It is designed and taught by Carbon Blue Global, an engineering services consultancy headquartered in Doha, Qatar. You learn from practising QS professionals and put the skills to work on live projects from the Middle East.

Who it is for

  • Civil engineering graduates and freshers aiming for an office-based QS career
  • Working civil engineers, such as site engineers, moving into quantity surveying, estimation or billing
  • QS and billing professionals who want structured training in POMI, FIDIC and Primavera P6

Eligibility: a background in civil engineering, good numeracy and basic computer skills.

How the course runs

  • 8 months, live online. 55 hours of technical sessions plus 3 hours of HR and CV sessions.
  • Classes on alternate days. Batches run Monday, Wednesday and Friday, or Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
  • Recorded sessions. Revisit topics at your own pace.
  • Help between classes. A WhatsApp doubt-clearance line for each topic, and one-to-one sessions with faculty on request.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the QS role, the main construction components and the project workflow from pre-contract to handover
  • Use Excel and AutoCAD for QS work, including taking off areas and perimeters from drawings
  • Measure works to POMI and prepare complete BOQs for civil, fit-out and roadworks, with specifications, RFIs and PlanSwift takeoffs
  • Prepare bar bending schedules (BBS) for footings and columns
  • Build unit rates through rate analysis, and prepare estimates and detailed budgets
  • Use value engineering to reduce cost while protecting function and quality
  • Work through tenders, contract types and FIDIC, GCC and SCC, work orders and purchase orders
  • Prepare RA bills, final bills and certificates of payment (COP), and adjust provisional sums and prime cost items
  • Identify, value and submit variations, and keep an accurate variation log
  • Prepare cash flow forecasts and cost reports, and manage manpower, materials and machinery (3M)
  • Plan and schedule projects in Primavera P6
  • Close out and hand over a project, and deliver a civil and fit-out BOQ on a Middle East live project
