# 3D ideas saved for the IOSH page (later)

Maasoom, 6 Oct 2026: "relevant for IOSH, keep them in the file, not for now". Proposed during the home-page
3D research (references in docs/ideas/home-night-sky.md and SPEC-home-night-sky.md section "3D").

- **The site builds itself in 360°.** During a scroll-driven orbit of a night construction site, the building
  assembles floor by floor in colour-coded BIM layers (structure off-white, MEP cyan/amber, finishes gold).
  References: Codrops "buildings wave"; Likova (videinfra.com/work/likova).
- **Safety zones on the orbit.** Hazard zones (crane swing radius, scaffold edge, excavation) glow amber one
  at a time as the camera passes, each with a short IOSH caption. Uses selective bloom (three.js example
  webgl_postprocessing_unreal_bloom_selective).
- **Holographic site screen.** A floating screen beside the site shows course stats or an LMS preview with
  soft scanlines (github.com/ektogamat/threejs-holographic-material).
