**Status:** DRAFT, 2 Oct 2026. Not live. A CBG trainer must approve or correct these before they go on the IOSH course page (SPEC 5.2, PLAN T22).
**Photo:** `brand/assets/photos/hazard-worksite.png` (AI-generated for CBG, staged on purpose).

On the course page, a gold scan line sweeps the photo and tags each hazard. On a laptop, students hover or tap a marker to read the label and the line under it. On a phone, they step through the hazards one at a time and the photo zooms in to each one.

**What I need from the trainer, for each hazard:** is the label correct and fair by OSH standards? Is the one-line explanation right? Change any wording. Students read English as a second language, so keep it short and plain.
